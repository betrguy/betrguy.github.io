Pegasus writer is a python script for making quick edits and creating new notes with the assistance of llama 3.1

It is still in it's early stages and these early notes are just a way to show progress/demonstrate functionality/ troubleshoot.

Right now I'm working out small issues in the user experience. The mission of Pegasus writer is to make writing notes more of a 'chat' experience and a 'collaboration effort' rather than just typing into the abyss.
