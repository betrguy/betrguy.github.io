---
title: Salt+Pepper Web Extension and MeatNet NOSTR Layer
---

I have been working on the salt+pepper web extension and the MeatNet NOSTR layer as a decentralized online 'Suite' for curating unique and meaningful versions of the internet. It's a Suite/tool that I want humans to use to document the best parts of the internet and attribute meaning to them, leaving future civilizations with a living, breathing, meaning-rich version of the internet. I want to liberate people and enable people to build a haven for connecting via ideas, sharing cool stuff, keeping notes in a new way... It's how something like the internet was always supposed to function. One day, maybe it will!
