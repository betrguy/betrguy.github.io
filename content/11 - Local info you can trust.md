---
title: 11 - Local info you can trust
---

In today's fragmented and decentralized world, it's more crucial than ever to have access to local information that you can trust. This is where Local Services Pages come in – a way for humans to broker trust and create digital real estate in the digital economy. By becoming an expert on the growing industries in your area, making pages for specific neighborhoods, and optimizing them for natural language search, you can establish yourself as a valued member of your community.

But how else can you contribute meaningful information instead of just consuming it? One approach is to create a directory or map of local resources, including reviews, ratings, and recommendations. This would allow users to find reliable sources of information and support local businesses. Another option is to develop a platform that connects people with similar interests or needs, fostering a sense of community and trust.

By doing so, you can become the go-to expert in your area, providing valuable insights and expertise that others can rely on. This not only helps build trust but also empowers individuals to take control of their online presence and create meaningful connections.
