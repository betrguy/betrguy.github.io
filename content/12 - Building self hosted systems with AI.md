---
title: 12 - Building self hosted systems with AI
---

It appears logical to me to use AI for augmenting your own intelligence first, before trying to automate the rest of the world. Much like an AI model seeks to self-optimize before attacking corporate profit pools, you should also self-optimize by building systems for news analysis/synthesis, tracker tools for the sectors you work in/study, local networks of competent people for the area you live in, offline tools for tasks you do every day... These are the logical starting points for someone looking to eventually wield AI as a profit/power engine. Start with self-transformation, optimization, and automation. Make it really easy for yourself to be who you are and do what you want to do. Make it very hard to not be yourself or do what you want to do. Build it into your systems. Create a positive (automated) feedback loop. Focus on automating your own competence first. Then work on whatever app or product/service you have ideas about.
