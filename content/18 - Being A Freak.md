title: 18 - Being A Freak
---

To me, being really selective and assuming a non-NPC, non accepting (skeptical) frame when I'm operating in most parts of life allows me to cut down on the choices. Simply cutting down on the number of choices can do a lot for making good choices. No I did not think the new movie looked good. No I did not think the new food item at the place was interesting. No I was not interested to learn more about that service. No I did not look at your social media photo. I didn't see about it and I also would not have liked it if I saw it. I don't want to run around trying the latest stuff and making comments and opinions on everything. I don't want a sea of options to choose from. I like to limit my choices by disliking most things. If I don't like something, it never enters my orbit in any meaningful way that can waste my time. If it never enter the orbit due to my selective taste, I can make better use of my time on the few things that are within my orbit. I use my taste as a tool to help control my orbit of things so that I can spend my time curating an orbit of things worth curating/ worth paying attention to. NPCs love everything. They think everything is awesome and the new thing is really cool and the expensive things are really worth striving for and new stuff just keeps popping up. It's a wonderful and retarded world and it just keeps getting better. Retard maxxing, when done right, can be more powerful than what I am doing. I use defensive measures like not enjoying most things that other people enjoy so that I can limit my time spent with people who like most things because they likely don't have good taste in most things. I use distaste as an equally important counterpart to my taste. Taste is a tool. For figuring out whether a thing is poisonous and will kill you or tasty and will keep you alive. To learn what food tastes bad (or isn't food) is to develop a key survival instinct. But taste is also a tool that works for beyond food. In the new world and the age of cognitive attacks and meaningless noise, taste can be used to figure out what will mentally drain you and kill you versus what will sustain you and grow you. Taste can be tailored to things that won't kill you and will grow you and your time can be spent on those things instead of everything.
