---
title: 20 - Humanity's Information Engine
---

The news cycle and narratives are a sort of version of reality in which we get to see pictures and words representing concepts come into the fold (and pass through and leave out of it) and do so sequentially, in a managed, controlled way. Now that it's not just a media monoculture comprised of a handful of TV/radio channels and sits in a somewhat distributed and decentralized state, the news is proving to be a useful source of connection to the world (as long as you can situate yourself within it carefully and not drown) or at least justifying its reason for existing, and forcing the monoculture/legacy news to keep up. While we usually aren't cognizant of it, we like the news created by the news cycle and we admit so by consuming so much of it and checking on it. It being the hivemind. Social media. The internet. AI chats. News. Videos. Podcasts. A tacit admission is an admission nonetheless and while we may never actually admit it, we enjoy consuming information. Specifically, a flow of information. We value novelty. Referred to as the "news cycle" we have invented a 24/7 engine of public discourse surrounding reality, connecting the world's population through common narratives. It is actually one of the more impressive technological accomplishments we have as a civ. It takes reality (time and space and matter) and passes it through a sort of funnel or lens, or some kind of informational bottleneck (censorship, bias, fear of public backlash, risk of sparking outrage, de-platforming, lost elections, falling stock valuations, financial interests, criminal charges, etc, etc) which creates a steady and even flow of information: stories, situations and narratives to shape the reality in which we live. It permeates stability for markets and economies and supply chains and governments and keeps humanity (mostly) moving at a calm, even pace. It is history, sifted and funneled through a series of modules and delivered through a broadcast to minds and hearts across the world. It is modern information alchemy. A steady flow of information which the markets live and die by, capital rotates and flees from it and preys and hunts around it, and people consume and are consumed by it. It is the phenomena of reality crystalizing into history like water turns to ice. Changing states of matter before our eyes as we witness transmutation of what was abstract to what is now concrete. What began as fleeting thoughts in the mind turned into physical realities, and when observed, solidified as truth. It may not be the actual truth, but it's the closest thing we have to a record of the truth, and I believe humanity's information engine is getting better with time.
