# How To Overcome Procrastination

I used to procrastinate on small tasks that my previous self perceived as big things. This was a major obstacle for me, and nothing seemed to work until I shifted my identity to a better version of myself who doesn't view these tasks as daunting. Now, all the tasks are pretty much done.

Before changing my identity, I tried various methods to overcome procrastination, but none were successful. However, assuming the frame of someone who doesn't procrastinate (because it's easier and better for me to just get it done) is how I got it done. The key lies in making this shift because I love myself not because I hate procrastinating. It comes from a good place. Before you can expect any changes in behavior, you first have to plant the seed into your identity and ideally, do some things from a place of genuine good intentions for the self.
