I'm working on the New World Grid. I am making my own news website for news that actually matters in a world where realities--ones that people previously 'shared' and mostly agreed upon under a monoculture--are rapidly fragmenting into thousands of sub-realities that don't really matter to you or me. There is not enough incentive for people to get along and agree (or agree to disagree) on trivial stuff for the sake maintaining civility within this highly contended new world. There is no incentive to agree with or to trust most people. That doesn't mean you should write them off entirely, however. Therein lies the subtlety of operating in the New World. 

I'm using quartz and HTML and CSS and starting to actually understand the instructions of the LLMs helping me build it (I am usually just copy pasting). I've gotten a fairly good grasp on building websites which won't exist in this capacity for much longer. 

The goal of the New World Grid is to build a network of competence that revolves around energy and parallel economies comprised of people and agents who truly believe in the emerging new world and truly benefit from doing the work/study.

I want to help liberate people in meaningful ways by breaking them out of background conditioning and Old World models of thought/analysis. Stuff like approaching markets with a New World perspective. Understanding that the old metrics for measuring the economy don't account for the new stuff driving the growth in new ways that are largely going undetected and presenting only in marketing hype, X threads and news cycles which fade quickly as a better story comes into view. It's a New World, in which attention is currency, and there is always a better story on the horizon.  

We don't benefit from the same incentives to live stably, seeking modest careers or foregoing risk in favor of a quiet life in a homogenous community. We as a generation look to occupy the top half of the K and join the 'treading on dividends' class to ensure survival. We look to ascend above downwardly-mobile peers who lack the cognitive security to avoid suffering from self-inflicted wounds caused by compulsive social media use. You don't want to think about your fellow man in that way and you don't want to view your fellow man as anything other than a peer. We are all willing to overlook certain qualities of our fellow man if it ensures that we can live prosperously in relative safety. The New World deems it necessary that your existence is secured firstly by your competence to act. It is a basic tenant of ensuring one's survival. In order to act effectively, you have to learn to first see the New World in the way which most benefits you, specifically. 

So how will you choose to view your fellow man? Is he cattle? Is he someone worth investing in? Is he deserving of a second chance? These are the questions we ask while operating in the New World.
