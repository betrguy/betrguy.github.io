I am making my own news website for news that actually matters in a world where realities (ones that were previously 'shared' and mostly agreed on) are fragmenting into thousands of sub-realities that don't really matter. Turns out there is not enough room for consensus within a highly contentious and competitive new world. I'm using quartz and HTML and CSS and actually understanding the instructions of the LLMs helping me build it (usually just copy pasting). The goal of this website is to build a network of competence that revolves around energy and parallel economies comprised of people who truly believe in the emerging new world and truly benefit from the work that I do. I want to help liberate people in meaningful and substantive ways by breaking them out of background conditioning and old world models of thought/analysis. We are living in the hour between midnight and dawn. The old world is dying. Its final, chaotic death-throes are the tremors you feel in your bank account and your courts.
