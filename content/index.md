# E.A.T. (Energy Acceleration Tracker) - A New Tool for the Domestic Energy Sector

The E.A.T. tool is designed to track the growth of the domestic energy sector in America by monitoring job listings, energy loan approvals, and other signals of breakthroughs/growth in American energy tech that would necessitate massive hiring waves for companies at the forefront of tech/defense.

This note serves as a starting point for documenting the E.A.T. tool.
