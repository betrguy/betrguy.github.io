---
title: 14 - Building small and local
---

New note about how a lot of people wonder what they can do to benefit from AI and the best answer I have is to make small and local stuff that you would use every day and it would actually save you time or keep you focused or keep you entertained or doing whatever you want to be doing. Make it easy for you to be yourself and do the things you like. Make it hard for yourself to not do life affirming things. Use automation as a tool to build more tools and make sure those tools are useful. For example, a program that allows you to describe 3D files and then build them in Python code, then converts to STL and prints in your printer.
